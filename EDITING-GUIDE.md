# Editing the AI Safety Hub Nigeria website

Everything on aisafetyhubng.com that changes often is edited in one place, the **Studio**:

**https://aisafetyhubng.sanity.studio**

Sign in with the account you were invited with. You never need to touch code.

## How it works

1. Open the Studio and pick what you want to change from the menu on the left.
2. Edit the fields. Every field has a short hint under it explaining what it does.
3. Click **Publish** (bottom right). Nothing goes live until you publish.
4. The website rebuilds by itself and shows your change within about **2 minutes**.

Made a mistake? Open the item, click the **⋯** menu next to Publish and use **Review changes** / history to restore an earlier version.

## What's in the menu

| Menu item | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Homepage and site settings** | The yellow "Happening now" bar, the big "13 fellows" number, contact email and location |
| **About page text** | Intro, Why AI safety in Nigeria, Our approach, vision, How we started, Support us |
| **Events** | Every event, upcoming and past (see below) |
| **Programmes** | APEx, reading group, talks, Global AI Watch: text, status, colour, links |
| **Team** | Name, role, short bio, photo, LinkedIn |
| **Partners** | Partner name, role, website and logo |
| **Quotes** | Quotes shown under "From our seminars" |
| **Timeline (About page)** | The clickable "Our story so far" timeline |

## Common tasks

### Change the "Happening now" bar
Homepage and site settings → **Happening now bar** tab → edit the title, details and link → Publish.
To hide it, untick **Show the "Happening now" bar**.

### Add an upcoming event
Events → **+ Create** → fill in:
- **Title** and **Date**. If the exact day isn't fixed yet, tick **Exact day not decided yet** and pick any day in that month.
- **Details line**, for example `Online · 90 minutes · free`
- **Registration link** (your Luma or Tally link)

Publish. It appears under "Next up" on the Events page and on the homepage.

### After an event happens
You don't need to move it: once the date passes it moves to "Previous events" automatically.
To make it look good there, open it and add:
- **Flyer or photo** (then drag the circle, the "hotspot", onto the most important part of the picture)
- **Card title** and **Card label** (for example `APEx seminar`)
- Tick **Show on the homepage after it has passed** if it should appear on the homepage too.

Events without a picture are listed under "Earlier".

### Add a team member
Team → **+ Create** → name, role, one or two sentence bio, a square headshot, LinkedIn link, and an **Order** number (lower numbers show first) → Publish.

### Update a programme's status
Programmes → pick the programme → change **Status (bold)** (for example `Applications open`) and **Status detail** (for example `Closes 15 January`) → Publish.

## Good habits

- **Photos:** use clear, well-lit images. Big files are fine; the site resizes them automatically.
- **Keep text short.** The website is designed for short lines, especially the homepage tiles.
- **Ask people first** before adding their photo, quote or name.
- Use **Publish**, not just save: drafts are never shown on the website.

## Things that are *not* in the Studio

These are rare changes; ask the person who manages the website code:
- Page layout and design, colours, fonts
- The APEx facts row and "Who APEx is for" list on the Programmes page
- The contact page's list of social links

Newsletter posts update automatically from Substack, and the calendar on the Events page comes straight from Luma.
